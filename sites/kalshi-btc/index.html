Overview

The Sniper Mindset

Like counting cards in blackjack. 95% of the time the deck is neutral and the bot sits out. 5% of the time the market drifts far enough from statistical reality that the bot fires a single aggressive limit order. If it goes hours without trading, that's not a bug — that's the strategy saving you from bad bets.

Compute

On every Coinbase tick (50ms throttle), computes fair probability via Normal CDF using rolling 30s realized vol. Scans all eligible markets across BTC, ETH, SOL, and XRP simultaneously.

Fire

When edge exceeds 10% after fees, places an aggressive limit order at ask + 2c. The matching engine gives best price regardless. One shot per market window, one direction per contract.

Hold

No exit management. Holds to settlement. The contract resolves at expiry. Win = $1 per contract minus entry cost. Lose = entry cost. Pure binary outcome, pure math.

Strategy

The Math

Each Kalshi contract is a binary bet: "Will BTC be above $X in 15 minutes?" The market prices this as a probability. The bot computes its own probability and only trades when the market is screamingly wrong.

PROBABILITY MODEL

z = (strike - spot) / (spot × realized_vol × √periods)
prob_yes = 1 - Φ(z)
Φ = standard Normal CDF
realized_vol = rolling std of 30s log returns (30-bar window)
periods = seconds_remaining / 30
spot = live Coinbase mid price per asset

EDGE CALCULATION

limit_price = ask + fill_buffer (2c)
fee = 0.07 × p × (1 - p)
edge = model_prob - (limit_price / 100) - fee
Edge computed at the actual ask — what you'd really pay to enter
Wide spreads naturally kill the edge. No separate spread filter needed
If edge < 10%: do nothing. The market isn't wrong enough

WHY DOES THE BOT USUALLY DO NOTHING?

1. Market is efficient. Model says 48%, market says 48c. Edge = 0. No trade. This is the most common state.
2. Spread kills the edge. Model says 60% fair value, but the ask is 62c. You'd be paying 62c for a 60c ticket. Negative edge after fees.
3. Edge too small. Model says 55%, ask is 47c. 8% edge doesn't clear the 10% threshold. A small edge can be erased by slippage or vol model error.
4. Fires when it matters. Model says 72%, ask is 45c. Edge = 23% after fees. One aggressive limit, wait for the fill.

Pipeline

Data Flow

Tick-driven architecture with 3-way fan-out. Every Coinbase trade fires the full pipeline in parallel.

Coinbase WS ticks (BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP)
├— CandleBuilder → 30s OHLCV → log returns → rolling vol → VolState
├— ChWriter → ClickHouse (tick persistence)
└— Evaluator → reads VolState + QuoteCache → Kalshi REST orders

Volatility Pipeline

• 30-second candles from raw Coinbase ticks
• Log returns between consecutive candle closes
• Rolling standard deviation over 30-bar window (15 min)
• Published via lock-free AtomicU64 — zero contention

Order Book Pipeline

• Kalshi WS orderbook_delta feed for live book updates
• Kalshi only exposes bids — asks are derived:
yes_ask = 100 - best_no_bid
• Requires live WS snapshot before trading any market
• Eager discovery predicts next ticker, pre-seeds cache
