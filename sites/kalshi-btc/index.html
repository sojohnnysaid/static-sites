Overview

How It Works

The sniper waits for the final seconds of each 15-minute window, when the vol model has maximum information and contracts are cheapest. Pure math, no heuristics.

Spot

Evaluates every 100ms starting 10s into each window. Computes P_fair from drift-aware log-normal model with EWMA vol. Looks for 7%+ edge after costs.

Fire

When edge exceeds 7% and spread is under 8c, places a limit order. $1 notional sizing. One trade per market, 15 trades/day max. ~50ms order latency.

Hold

No exit management. Holds to settlement. The contract resolves in under 2 minutes. Win = $1 per contract, lose = entry cost. Pure binary outcome.

Strategy

The Math

Drift-aware log-normal digital option pricing. No signal biases bolted on — just the vol model and a skew adjustment.

CORE MODEL

P_fair = 1 - Φ((ln(S0/St) - μτ + 0.5σ2τ) / (σ√τ))
σ = EWMA realized vol per 10s bar (λ=0.97)
μ = drift (EMA of recent log returns, 12-bar lookback)
τ = time remaining in bar units
skew_beta = 0.40 (log-moneyness adjustment)

WHAT'S STRIPPED

Momentum bias Book imbalance Funding rate bias Trade flow bias Micro-vol bias ML meta-model Active exits Dynamic edge threshold

WHY NEAR EXPIRY?

1. Maximum information. With 13+ minutes of price action observed, the vol model's estimate of P_fair is most accurate.
2. Cheapest contracts. If one side is winning, the losing side trades at deep discounts (5-30c). Small edge = large % return.
3. Minimal time risk. Only 2 minutes to settlement. Less time for the market to move against the position.
